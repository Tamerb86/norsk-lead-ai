# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Norwegian B2B sales and marketing people at small and mid-sized companies who do
outbound lead generation. They open the app to find companies (from the Brønnøysund
business registry), enrich contacts, run cold-email campaigns, and now triage and
follow up on replies through the AI follow-up agent. Their context is daily,
task-focused work — often several short sessions a day — where speed, clarity, and
trust in the data matter more than visual spectacle. Norwegian-language UI throughout.

## Product Purpose

Help Norwegian companies turn the public business registry into qualified, contacted
leads with as little manual effort as possible: search → enrich → campaign → reply
agent. Success is the operator spending less time on prospecting busywork and more
on real conversations — measured in leads contacted, replies handled, and meetings
booked, not in time spent inside the tool.

## Brand Personality

Professional, confident, calm. The interface should feel like a serious, dependable
work instrument for B2B sales — precise, well-organized, quietly competent. Voice is
plain and direct (Norwegian bokmål), never hype-y or playful. It earns trust by being
legible, fast, and predictable; the intelligence (AI enrichment, reply classification,
drafting) shows through accurate, useful output rather than flashy presentation.

## Anti-references

- **Generic washed-out SaaS**: the recurring gray/violet template, identical card
  grids, the unmistakable "AI made this" look. Avoid the monoculture.
- **Crowded dashboards**: excessive data density, charts and metrics with no focus or
  hierarchy — visual fatigue.
- **Loud / overhyped**: gradient text, heavy glow, exaggerated motion, shouting color.
  Erodes trust in a work tool.
- **Slow / heavy**: large imagery and weighty graphics that drag daily, repeated use.

## Design Principles

1. **Clarity over density.** Every screen has one primary task; surface it first and
   let secondary information recede. Resist the urge to show everything at once.
2. **Earn trust through legibility and speed.** High-contrast, readable text; fast,
   predictable interactions. A work tool that is calm to read is a tool people trust.
3. **Let the intelligence speak through accuracy, not decoration.** AI features prove
   themselves with correct, well-labeled output and clear confidence signals — never
   with visual spectacle.
4. **Quiet confidence.** Restraint is the brand. One deliberate accent, generous
   structure, no ornament that doesn't carry information.
5. **Safe by default in outward actions.** Anything that contacts a real lead is
   reviewable and reversible in the UI; the design makes the safe path the easy path.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target **WCAG 2.1 AA** — also a legal expectation in Norway (universell utforming /
forskrift om universell utforming av IKT) for digital services. Body text ≥ 4.5:1
contrast, large text ≥ 3:1; visible keyboard focus; semantic structure and labels for
screen readers; never rely on color alone to convey meaning (pair the classification/
sentiment colors with text). Honor `prefers-reduced-motion` on every animation.
