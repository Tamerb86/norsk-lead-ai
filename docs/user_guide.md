# NorskLeads User Guide

Welcome to the NorskLeads user guide. This document will help you get started with the platform and use its features effectively.

## 1. Getting Started

### 1.1. Registration

To start using NorskLeads, you need to create an account. You can register using your email and password, or with your Google or Microsoft account.

### 1.2. Onboarding

After registration, you will be guided through a short onboarding process to set up your account and preferences.

## 2. Core Features

### 2.1. Dashboard

The dashboard provides an overview of your activities, including campaign statistics, recent leads, and upcoming tasks.

### 2.2. Company Search

You can search for companies using various filters such as location, industry, number of employees, and more. The search results provide detailed information about each company.

### 2.3. Campaigns

Create and manage email campaigns to reach out to your leads. You can use templates, schedule your campaigns, and track their performance.

### 2.4. Leads

Manage your leads, track their status, and view their interaction history with your campaigns.

### 2.5. Templates

Create and manage email templates to use in your campaigns. You can personalize your templates with dynamic variables.

## 3. Advanced Features

### 3.1. A/B Testing

Create A/B tests for your email campaigns to find out what works best. You can test different subjects, content, and sending times.

### 3.2. Lead Scoring

Set up lead scoring rules to automatically rank your leads based on their engagement and attributes. This helps you focus on the most promising leads.

### 3.3. Webhooks

Integrate NorskLeads with other applications using webhooks. You can receive real-time notifications about events such as new leads, campaign updates, and more.

## 4. Settings

### 4.1. Account Settings

Manage your account information, password, and notification preferences.

### 4.2. Team Settings

If you are part of a team, you can manage your team members and their roles.

### 4.3. AI Settings

Configure the AI settings to get personalized suggestions and insights.

## 5. Support

If you need help, you can access the help center or contact our support team through the platform.
